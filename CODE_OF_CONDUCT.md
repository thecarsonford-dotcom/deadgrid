# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want DEADGRID to be a welcoming, practical place to build and learn. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and make room for people with different experience levels, hardware, languages, and viewpoints.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and personal attacks are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome; hostility toward people is not.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository Issues, pull requests, Discussions, project spaces, and public spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and patterns of behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public Issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as reasonably possible.

Maintainers must avoid conflicts of interest and should ask another trusted maintainer to review a report when appropriate.
